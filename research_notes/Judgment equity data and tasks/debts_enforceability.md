# Debts and enforceability: free judgment, lien and tax-warrant sources, and the 50-state judgment-life rules (as of 2026-11-25)

Scope note. The repo already holds most free sources (config/liens/ has 317 configs; reports/More free judgment sources.md and reports/Republished court judgment datasets.md rank them). It also holds config/sol_proposed.yaml, an unverified 50-state draft built from the White and Williams 2020 chart. These notes add only what is new: sources not yet in the repo, scope changes to sources already in it, clerk bulk-data prices, and a check of the 50-state rules against primary statute text. Where a primary text contradicts sol_proposed.yaml, the contradiction is listed.

Method and access limits. WebFetch could not resolve many hosts, including library.nclc.org, ezel.ai, bpd.cdn.sos.ca.gov and ilga.gov. Shell `curl` did reach most state legislature sites. Each "V" row below was read from the official statute page on 2026-11-25. These hosts were blocked or refused (403/429/turnstile): leginfo.legislature.ca.gov, flsenate.gov, nysenate.gov, docs.legis.wisconsin.gov, cga.ct.gov, code.wvlegislature.gov, oscn.net, capitol.hawaii.gov, legis.la.gov (JS) and statutes.capitol.texas.gov (JS). The rules for those states are therefore unverified ("U"). No paid or login-gated source was used.

## Q1. Which states and counties publish civil judgment indexes as open data or free bulk files, and which clerks sell bulk data at what price?

### Takeaway
No new free, named, statewide private civil-judgment index turned up beyond what the repo already has. A sweep of the Socrata catalog for "judgment", "abstract of judgment", "garnishment" and "civil case" returned only administrative, agency and aggregate sets. Private judgments still come from paid clerk subscriptions or records requests. Florida clerks publish the clearest prices: roughly $350 to $2,700 a year per data feed, or $75 to $110 a month per court division or folder.

### Cited Findings
- A Socrata catalog search (api.us.socrata.com) for "judgment", "judgement", "abstract of judgment", "garnishment" and "civil case" found no court money-judgment index with debtor names. Hits were limited to the following. TCEQ Civil Judgments (`data.texas.gov/7ift-kbgd`) is already in the repo. NOLA code-enforcement hearings (`44ct-56tr`, `uzyk-jrck`) are already in the repo as la.nola.code_hearing_judgments. Texas OCA landlord/tenant caseloads (`8qme-eqs9`, `t2fh-6f7n`) are aggregate counts only. NYC Law Department "Case-Related Information About Civil Litigation" (`pjgc-h7uv`) lists cases against the City, with payout and disposition amounts and a Lead BBL; it is not a debtor list. Cthru "Settlements and Judgments" (`gpqz-7ppn`) covers state payouts. — [Socrata catalog API](https://api.us.socrata.com/api/catalog/v1?q=judgment&only=dataset); [pjgc-h7uv](https://data.cityofnewyork.us/d/pjgc-h7uv)
- Polk County FL Clerk sells court bulk data as daily files over secure FTP. The price is $75 a month per court division, plus $100 a year per division for historical files. Buyers sign notarized subscription and bulk-data agreements. — [Polk Clerk bulk data](https://pro.polkcountyclerk.net/PRR/Home/BulkDataSubscriber)
- Miami-Dade Clerk Commercial Data Services charges $110 a month for each court data folder, plus $420 a month for Official Records images over FTP. — [Miami-Dade Clerk](https://www.miamidadeclerk.gov/clerk/commercial-data-services.page)

  - Court data files: $75 setup per file plus $350 a year per file.
  - Sources: [Lake OR agreement PDF](https://www.lakecountyclerkfl.gov/wp-content/uploads/OfficialRecordsSubscriberAgreement.pdf); [Lake OR subscriptions](https://www.lakecountyclerkfl.gov/departments/records-administrative-services/official-records/recording/subscription-services-official-records/); [Lake court subscriptions](https://www.lakecountyclerkfl.gov/departments/courts-management/court-data-records-division/subscription-services-courts/)

### Inferences
- Florida's 67 clerks are the most likely paid path to recorded judgments. A per-county Official Records index costs about $375 to $2,660 a year. Statewide Florida coverage would cost tens of thousands of dollars a year, so a free Chapter 119 request for an existing index export, at duplication cost, remains the cheaper path. The repo's "Recorder data records laws by state" report covers that statute.
- Ohio is unusual. Judgment liens are filed as certificates of judgment with the common pleas clerk (R.C. 2329.02, see Q5), not with the recorder. The Ohio judgment-lien index therefore lives in court-clerk systems, and a Public Records Act request should go to the clerk of courts, not the recorder.

### Gaps
- None of the clerk prices was confirmed on the clerk page itself: WebFetch was blocked and the search tool returned summaries. Treat every price as unverified until the page is opened.
- I found no current price list for Texas JP extracts, the Cook County Clerk, Maricopa Superior Court or the Pennsylvania AOPC in this pass.

## Q2. Which states publish tax-warrant or delinquent-taxpayer lists as structured data rather than PDF?

### Takeaway
Only New York (tax warrants and child-support warrants, with name, address, amount, filed and expiration dates and status) and Vermont (top 100) publish structured, API-ready state tax-debtor lists. The NY datasets were re-created in mid-2025 and are now titled "Beginning 7/1/2025". The repo's configs still label them "Beginning 1982" and "Beginning 2004", so their scope needs checking. Missouri's child-support lien list is structured but has no names, only VINs. Most other states post HTML or PDF pages with dollar thresholds.

### Cited Findings
- **NY tax warrants (`data.ny.gov/d/v7ua-z23v`).** Titled "New York State Tax Warrants: Beginning 7/1/2025", created 2025-06-26 and updated 2026-11-25. Description: "NYS Tax warrants and related satisfactions, vacates or amendments filed by the NYS Department of Tax and Finance with the NYS Department of State." Columns: Debtor Name 1–4, Debtor Address 1–2, City, State, Zip, County Code, Warrant ID, Warrant filed amount, Warrant filed date, Warrant expiration date, Status Code (A/C/U), and satisfaction, vacate and amend dates. — [Socrata catalog record](https://api.us.socrata.com/api/catalog/v1?ids=v7ua-z23v)
- **NY child-support warrants (`8pp4-isha`).** Titled "New York State Child Support Warrants: Beginning 7/1/2025", created 2025-07-11 and updated daily. It has the same fields with a single Debtor Name. — [Socrata catalog record](https://api.us.socrata.com/api/catalog/v1?ids=8pp4-isha)
- The repo's configs reference these same IDs under the older titles "Tax-Warrants-Beginning-1982" and "Child-Support-Warrants-Beginning-2004". — repo `config/liens/ny.dtf.tax_warrants.yaml`, `config/liens/ny.dtf.child_support_warrants.yaml`
- **Missouri Child Support Lien List (`data.mo.gov/d/7t5a-79ri`).** Updated 2026-09-11. Its only columns are Child Support Lien Date, Year, Make, Lien Satisfaction Date and VIN Number. It has no obligor name or address, so it cannot be joined to people except through a VIN-to-owner lookup, which the Driver's Privacy Protection Act (DPPA) restricts. — [Socrata catalog record](https://api.us.socrata.com/api/catalog/v1?ids=7t5a-79ri)
- **Vermont.** Top 100 Businesses (`ag2q-tdje`) and Top 100 Individuals (`b5xi-3qaq`) on data.vermont.gov, updated 2026-11-23. Both are already in the repo. — [Socrata catalog](https://api.us.socrata.com/api/catalog/v1?q=delinquent%20taxpayer&only=dataset)
- **Wisconsin DOR.** An HTML list page, no CSV link found in the page HTML. A taxpayer is posted when they owe more than $5,000 (tax, interest, penalty, fees and costs) unpaid more than 90 days after appeal rights expire. Sellers with revoked permits are posted under sec. 73.03(64), Wis. Stats., effective 2009-10-01. People on payment plans, with an offer in compromise or in bankruptcy are excluded. — [WI DOR delinquent taxpayers](https://www.revenue.wi.gov/Pages/HTML/delqlist.aspx)
- **Oregon DOR.** Discloses taxpayers owing at least $50,000 in unresolved delinquent tax, after mailed notice. The page cites the statute as "305.806" in the extracted text; the cite is unverified. The only document link in the page HTML was a PDF (`/dor/Documents/110-033 CL2.pdf`); no CSV was found. — [OR DOR delinquent list](https://www.oregon.gov/dor/programs/collections/pages/delinquent-taxpayers.aspx)
- **Florida DOR.** Monthly Excel file of taxpayers with unresolved warrants or liens of $100,000 or more. Already in the repo as fl.dor.tax_warrants. — [FL DOR](https://floridarevenue.com/taxes/compliance/Pages/delinquent_taxpayer.aspx)
- **Texas Comptroller.** Paid-up and delinquent reports go only to local taxing jurisdictions, on request. — [TX Comptroller by-request reports](https://comptroller.texas.gov/transparency/open-data/by-request.php)
- **Nebraska and South Carolina.** Both publish web lists; Nebraska's is already in the repo and South Carolina's is listed as needing allowlisting. — [NE DOR](https://revenue.nebraska.gov/about/nebraska-delinquent-taxpayer-list); [SCDOR Jan 2025](https://dor.sc.gov/news/scdor-updates-top-delinquent-taxpayers-list-january-2025)
- **Massachusetts DOR.** The delinquent-taxpayer page returned 403 to the shell. — [mass.gov](https://www.mass.gov/info-details/dor-list-of-delinquent-taxpayers)

### Inferences
- The "Beginning 7/1/2025" titles suggest DTF may have restarted both NY series from mid-2025, which would drop historical open warrants. The adapter should check the minimum `Warrant filed date` before treating the set as complete. The older series may still exist under other IDs; this is unverified.
- Missouri's list is useful only as a "this VIN carries a child-support lien" flag. It should not be added as a debtor source.

### Gaps
- I did not confirm the file format of the Wisconsin, Oregon, South Carolina, Nebraska, Massachusetts, Kentucky, Indiana, Georgia or Illinois lists (HTML table, PDF or XLSX) in this pass.
- I did not check DOR lists for AL, AR, CO, CT, IA, ID, KS, LA, MD, ME, MN, MS, NC, NJ, NM, OK or UT.

## Q3. Is there free federal tax lien bulk data from state UCC and lien filing offices?

### Takeaway
Yes, in a few states. Connecticut publishes IRS liens nightly as public-domain open data. Colorado publishes UCC filings, including liens, daily, but the debtor name sits in a separate table. California reportedly gives weekly UCC data free (unverified). Idaho and Kentucky charge or require a login. In most states, federal tax liens against individuals' real property are filed with county recorders, not the Secretary of State, so a UCC feed covers mainly business debtors.

### Cited Findings
- **Connecticut, "Uniform Commercial Code (UCC) Lien Filings" (`data.ct.gov/d/xfev-8smz`).** Public Domain, updated 2026-11-25. Description: "Most liens are filed under the Uniform Commercial Code, but the dataset also contains vessel and aircraft liens, and liens filed by the Internal Revenue Service, the Connecticut Department of Labor, and municipalities." It holds only active liens, plus those under one year past lapse, and terminated liens stay for one year. A lapse date of 9999-12-31 means no lapse. Fields include Lien Type, Filing Type, Lien Status, debtor and secured-party names and addresses, and filing and lapse dates. Already in the repo as ct.sots.liens. — [Socrata catalog record](https://api.us.socrata.com/api/catalog/v1?ids=xfev-8smz); [CT business.ct.gov bulk data](https://business.ct.gov/knowledge-base/articles/sots/bulk-data-and-images)
- **Colorado Department of State.** Publishes UCC Filing Information (`wffy-3uut`), Debtor Information (`8upq-58vz`), Collateral (`4am6-w6u4`), Secured Party (`ap62-sav4`) and an EFS farm-products list (`ej2c-jkvh`), all Public Domain and updated 2026-11-23. The filing table "includes UCC financing statements, effective financing statements (EFS), and liens", with documentType, filingType, lapseDate and terminationFlag. Already in the repo as co.sos.ucc. — [Socrata catalog record](https://api.us.socrata.com/api/catalog/v1?ids=wffy-3uut)
- **Oregon SOS.** "UCC List of Filings Entered Last Month" (`snfi-f79b`) and "UCC Secured Parties List" (`2kf7-i54h`). Already in the repo as or.sos.ucc. — [Socrata catalog](https://api.us.socrata.com/api/catalog/v1?q=ucc&only=dataset)
- **California SOS.** Reported to provide weekly UCC data and images at no charge, with a $100 master unload of the full file. This comes from a search summary of the CA SOS fee schedule; the PDF host was not reachable, so it is unverified. — [CA SOS UCC fee schedule](https://bpd.cdn.sos.ca.gov/ucc/ucc-fee-schedule.pdf); [CA bizfile help](https://bpd.cdn.sos.ca.gov/ucc/ucc-online-help.pdf)
- **Idaho SOS.** Releases UCC and lien data in bulk every two weeks at $125 per file download (search summary). — [Idaho SOS UCC](https://sos.idaho.gov/ucc/)
- **Other states.** Kentucky SOS bulk data is a monthly full file for logged-in subscribers only. Arkansas sells a "UCC Bulk Records Data Download" through its portal (price not read). South Dakota offers database downloads by subscription form. Maine runs UCC online services, with bulk terms not read. — [KY SOS](https://www.sos.ky.gov/bus/Pages/Bulk-Data-Service.aspx); [Arkansas.gov](https://portal.arkansas.gov/service/ar-ucc-bulk-records-data-download/); [SD SOS](https://sdsos.gov/Business-Services/database-downloads.aspx); [Maine SOS](https://www.maine.gov/sos/corporations-commissions/uniform-commercial-code/uniform-commercial-code-online-services)

### Inferences
- California's weekly UCC feed is the largest possible new free lien source, if the "no charge" reading holds. Whether it carries federal tax lien notices for business debtors needs checking: under the Uniform Federal Lien Registration Act, federal tax liens against corporations and partnerships are usually filed with the Secretary of State. This rule comes from general knowledge and has not been checked state by state.
- For individual debtors, IRS liens are recorded with county recorders in most states, so the free paths are county recorder indexes such as NYC ACRIS (already loaded) and the Cook and Philadelphia sets already in the repo.

### Gaps
- I did not check whether California's free weekly UCC files include the federal or state tax lien document types, or whether a login or agreement is needed. The host was blocked.
- I did not survey which states file federal tax liens against individuals with the SOS. Under the Uniform Federal Lien Registration Act, a few states do; the list is unverified.

## Q4. Which compilations of judgment duration and renewal by state are authoritative, how current are they, and what do the primary statutes say in 2026?

### Takeaway
No current, authoritative, machine-readable 50-state table exists. The White and Williams chart (rev. 2020-01-20), which the repo already uses, is the best free compilation, but it is out of date for at least Virginia (2021), North Dakota (2021), California (2023–2024), Utah (2025) and Illinois (2026). I checked 25 jurisdictions against official statute text on 2026-11-25. Of those, Virginia and North Dakota contradict config/sol_proposed.yaml outright. Several others need extra fields: Arizona's medical-debt interest rate, Washington's consumer-debt rate, Michigan's re-record rule, Nevada's 6-year lien and Kansas's relation-back to the petition date.

### Cited Findings — compilations and their reliability
- **White and Williams LLP, "Judgment Lien Duration, Expiration, Renewal or Revival".** Rev. 2020-01-20, 187 text lines, 50 states plus DC. It cites statutes but carries the disclaimer "Certain exceptions may apply and law is subject to change." It already records Arizona's 2018 change to 10 years. — [W&W PDF](https://www.whiteandwilliams.com/assets/htmldocuments/Judgment%20Liens%20-%20Rev%201-20-20.PDF)
- **Bernstein Law Firm, "50-State Judgment Renewal Timing".** Dated 2016, older than W&W. — [Bernstein PDF](https://bernsteinlaw.com/wp-content/uploads/2016/01/50-State-Judgment-Renewal-Timing.pdf)
- **Secondary aggregators** (ezel.ai survey, usassetrecords.com, worldpopulationreview.com "Judgment Expiration by State 2026"). Unsourced or garbled in places; the search tool flagged the ezel Utah entry as garbled. Use only as leads. — [ezel.ai](https://ezel.ai/surveys/judgment-lien-duration-renewal); [usassetrecords](https://usassetrecords.com/how-long-is-a-judgment-good-for/); [WPR](https://worldpopulationreview.com/state-rankings/judgment-expiration-by-state)
- **NCLC.** "No Fresh Start 2025" (homestead and exemption tables, already cited in sol_proposed.yaml) and "New Consumer Law Changes Taking Effect in 2026". The 2026 page was not reachable (DNS blocked). — [NCLC 2026 changes](https://library.nclc.org/article/new-consumer-law-changes-taking-effect-2026)

### Cited Findings — law changed since the W&W chart (2020)
- **Virginia (2021).** For judgments dated on or after 2021-07-01, no execution may issue after 10 years. Judgments dated earlier keep 20 years. Child-support judgments dated on or after 2021-07-01 keep 20 years. The 10-year period can be extended by recording a certificate in the clerk's office before it expires, which adds 10 years; one more certificate adds 10 more, for a maximum of 30 years. This contradicts sol_proposed.yaml ("Enforceable 20 yrs; extend by motion"). — [Va. Code § 8.01-251](https://law.lis.virginia.gov/vacode/title8.01/chapter3/section8.01-251/)
- **North Dakota (2021).** For judgments docketed after 2021-08-01, the lien lasts 20 years from docketing in the county of rendition, and the judgment "must be canceled of record" after 20 years. Renewal by affidavit (§ 28-20-21) applies only to judgments docketed before 2021-08-01, and that section is repealed effective 2031-08-01. Older judgments keep a 10-year lien, renewable once to 20. This contradicts sol_proposed.yaml ("Lien 10 yrs; renew by affidavit"). — [N.D.C.C. ch. 28-20 (§§ 28-20-13, -21, -35)](https://ndlegis.gov/cencode/t28c20.pdf)
- **Illinois (2026).** Public Act 104-0120 (SB 1738), signed 2025-08-01 and effective 2026-01-01, amends 735 ILCS 5/2-1602. A consumer-debt judgment entered on or after the effective date "may not be revived but may be enforceable for a period of 15 years after its entry". Consumer-debt judgments entered from 2020-01-01 to the effective date may be revived only by a petition filed within 10 years of entry. The act also adds a $1,000 personal-property equity exemption on consumer-debt judgments. — [P.A. 104-0120](https://ilga.gov/documents/legislation/PublicActs/104/104-0120.htm); [SB1738 status](https://ilga.gov/ftp/legislation/104/BillStatus/HTML/10400SB1738.html); [735 ILCS 5/2-1602](https://lrb.ilga.gov/legislation/ilcs/documents/073500050K2-1602.htm)
- **Utah (2025).** S.B. 326, enrolled 2025 General Session, amends §§ 78B-2-311, 78B-5-202, 78B-6-1802 and -1804. Judgments continue 8 years from entry. A renewal order "maintains the date of the original judgment", "maintains the priority of collection" and restarts the limitation period. A judgment "may be renewed multiple times", and each renewal runs 8 years from the renewal order. One aggregator claims a further 2026 amendment; that claim is unverified. — [Utah S.B. 326 enrolled](https://le.utah.gov/Session/2025/bills/enrolled/SB0326.pdf)
- **California (2023–2024).** SB 1200, Ch. 883, Stats. 2022, effective 2023-01-01, changed interest on money judgments against natural persons (previously a flat 10%) and limited renewal of certain medical-expense and personal-debt judgments (CCP §§ 683.110, 685.010). The Judicial Council revised forms EJ-190 and EJ-195. CCP § 683.110 was amended again by Stats. 2023 ch. 131 (AB 1754), effective 2024-01-01. The exact consumer-debt interest rate and renewal bar were NOT confirmed because leginfo returned 403. — [SB 1200 chaptered (LegiScan)](https://legiscan.com/CA/text/SB1200/2021); [Judicial Council SPR23-09](https://www4.courts.ca.gov/documents/spr23-09.pdf); [CCP 683.110 (secondary)](https://rulesofcivilprocedure.com/ca/section-683.110/)
- **Arizona (2018 and 2022).** Laws 2018, ch. 36 (HB 2240), effective 2018-08-03, moved the execution and renewal periods from 5 to 10 years. Under § 12-1551(D), the 10-year rule applies to judgments entered on or after 2013-08-03, and to earlier judgments only if they were renewed by 2018-08-02. Interest on medical-debt judgments is the lesser of the weekly 1-year constant-maturity Treasury yield or 3% (§ 44-1201). Other judgments carry the lesser of 10% or prime plus 1%, fixed at entry. — [A.R.S. § 12-1551](https://www.azleg.gov/ars/12/01551.htm); [A.R.S. § 44-1201](https://www.azleg.gov/ars/44/01201.htm); [Laws 2018 ch. 36](https://www.azleg.gov/legtext/53leg/2R/laws/0036.htm)
- **Maryland (pending rule).** In June 2025 the Rules Committee approved amendments to Rules 2-625 and 3-625 that would allow only one further 12-year renewal (24 years maximum) for judgments entered after the rule takes effect. The later 227th Report still lists them as proposed, so adoption and the effective date are unconfirmed. Separately, CJP § 11-402 now requires a complaint to state whether the judgment sought is for medical debt. — [Rules Committee minutes 2025-06-26](https://www.mdcourts.gov/sites/default/files/minutes-rules/minutes20250626.pdf); [227th Report](https://www.mdcourts.gov/sites/default/files/rules/reports/227threport.pdf); [CJP § 11-402](https://mgaleg.maryland.gov/mgawebsite/Laws/StatuteText?article=gcj&section=11-402)
- **Washington (pending bill).** SB 5720 (2025–26), a "uniform consumer debt default judgments" act. No evidence it has been enacted. — [SB 5720](https://lawfilesext.leg.wa.gov/biennium/2025-26/Pdf/Bills/Senate%20Bills/5720-S.pdf)
- **Ohio.** R.C. 2329.07 is effective 2017-04-06 (SB 227, 131st GA). R.C. 2329.02 was amended by SB 94 (135th GA), effective 2024-10-24. — [R.C. 2329.07](https://codes.ohio.gov/ohio-revised-code/section-2329.07); [R.C. 2329.02](https://codes.ohio.gov/ohio-revised-code/section-2329.02)

## Q5. How does a judgment lien attach to real property in each state, docketing versus recording an abstract, and therefore where is an index needed?

### Takeaway
Attachment splits about evenly. In docketing states the court clerk's judgment docket is itself the lien index, so court data is enough: NC, SC, SD, ND, MN, IA, KS, MO, NE, WA, VA, MD, DE, IN, WI, NY and NJ (the last five unverified). In recording states the lien exists only once an abstract, certified copy, transcript or notice is recorded with the county recorder, so a recorder index is needed: AZ, ID, NV, UT, MI, VT, DC and, from the repo proposal (unverified), CA, CO, FL, IL, TX, TN, AL, GA, KY, OK, WY, CT and NM. Ohio is a third type: a certificate of judgment filed with the common pleas clerk. Maine, Massachusetts, New Hampshire and Rhode Island have no general judgment lien; the creditor must levy on or attach the property.

### Cited Findings
- **Arizona (recording).** The lien arises only on recording a certified copy with the county recorder "in each county where the judgment creditor desires the judgment to become a lien", together with an information statement (§ 33-967). "Failure to substantially comply ... results in the judgment not becoming a lien." — [A.R.S. § 33-961](https://www.azleg.gov/ars/33/00961.htm)
- **Idaho (recording).** "From the time of such recording, and not before, the judgment so recorded becomes a lien." — [Idaho Code § 10-1110](https://legislature.idaho.gov/statutesrules/idstat/Title10/T10CH11/SECT10-1110/)
- **Nevada (recording).** A transcript, abstract or certified copy "may be recorded in the office of the county recorder in any county, and when so recorded it becomes a lien". — [NRS 17.150](https://www.leg.state.nv.us/NRS/NRS-017.html)
- **Utah (recording since 2002).** After 2002-07-01, district and justice court judgments become liens only if the judgment or an abstract, with debtor-identifying information, is recorded with the county recorder. Before 1997-07-01, entry alone created a lien in the county. — [Utah S.B. 326 enrolled (78B-5-202(2),(7))](https://le.utah.gov/Session/2025/bills/enrolled/SB0326.pdf)
- **Michigan (recording).** A notice of judgment lien is recorded with the register of deeds. The lien expires 5 years after recording and may be re-recorded once. — [MCL 600.2809](https://www.legislature.mi.gov/Laws/MCL?objectName=mcl-600-2809)
- **Vermont (recording).** The judgment lien is recorded in the land records and lasts 8 years. — [12 V.S.A. § 2903](https://legislature.vermont.gov/statutes/section/12/113/02903)
- **DC (recording).** The judgment, when "filed and recorded in the office of the Recorder of Deeds", is a lien on freehold and leasehold estates. — [D.C. Code § 15-102](https://code.dccouncil.gov/us/dc/council/code/sections/15-102)
- **Maine (execution filing).** The lien arises by filing the execution, or an attested copy, with the registry of deeds within 3 years after it issues. Filing with the Secretary of State does the same for personal property. — [14 M.R.S. § 4651-A](https://legislature.maine.gov/statutes/14/title14sec4651-A.html)
- **North Carolina (docketing).** The clerk indexes and records the judgment on the county judgment docket. Other counties are reached by filing a transcript. The lien is effective against third parties from indexing and lasts 10 years from entry. — [G.S. 1-234](https://www.ncleg.gov/EnactedLegislation/Statutes/HTML/BySection/Chapter_1/GS_1-234.html)
- **South Carolina (docketing).** The lien begins on entry in the book of abstracts of judgments in any county and lasts 10 years from the judgment. — [S.C. Code § 15-35-810](https://www.scstatehouse.gov/code/t15c035.php)
- **Minnesota (docketing).** "From the time of docketing the judgment is a lien ... upon all real property in the county", but "not a lien upon registered land unless it is also recorded". — [Minn. Stat. § 548.09](https://www.revisor.mn.gov/statutes/cite/548.09)
- **South Dakota (docketing).** The lien attaches on docketing with the clerk of the circuit court, except on the homestead, for 10 years. — [SDCL 15-16-7](https://sdlegislature.gov/api/Statutes/15-16-7.html)
- **North Dakota (docketing).** The clerk dockets the judgment in the "judgment docket", and other counties by transcript. The homestead is excluded. — [N.D.C.C. § 28-20-13](https://ndlegis.gov/cencode/t28c20.pdf)
- **Iowa (judgment).** District-court and federal judgments are liens on the defendant's real estate for 10 years. Homesteads are excluded except as § 561.21 provides. — [Iowa Code § 624.23](https://www.legis.iowa.gov/docs/code/624.23.pdf)
- **Kansas (judgment).** The judgment is a lien in the county of rendition, effective from petition filing but not more than 4 months before judgment. — [K.S.A. 60-2202](https://www.ksrevisor.gov/statutes/chapters/ch60/060_022_0002.html)
- **Missouri (judgment).** Supreme, federal, appellate, circuit and probate judgments are liens in the county. Associate, small-claims and municipal division judgments are excluded from automatic lien. — [RSMo 511.350](https://revisor.mo.gov/main/OneSection.aspx?section=511.350)
- **Washington (docketing).** A superior court judgment's lien commences "from the time of the filing by the county clerk upon the execution docket". A federal judgment's lien commences from entry or filing in the county. — [RCW 4.56.200](https://app.leg.wa.gov/RCW/default.aspx?cite=4.56.200)
- **Virginia (docketing).** The lien runs "from the time such judgment is recorded on the judgment lien docket of the clerk's office of the county or city where such land is situated". The circuit court clerk keeps that docket, so a judgment from another county must be docketed there. — [Va. Code § 8.01-458](https://law.lis.virginia.gov/vacode/title8.01/chapter17/section8.01-458/)
- **Maryland (court index).** A money judgment is a lien from its date on land in the county of rendition "if indexed and recorded as prescribed by the Maryland Rules". — [CJP § 11-402(c)](https://mgaleg.maryland.gov/mgawebsite/Laws/StatuteText?article=gcj&section=11-402)
- **Delaware (court entry).** "A judgment shall bind lands only from the time of actually entering or signing it." Superior Court liens last 10 years. — [10 Del. C. §§ 4702, 4711](https://delcode.delaware.gov/title10/c047/sc01/index.html)
- **Ohio (certificate with court clerk).** A judgment of any court of general jurisdiction, including federal district courts, is a lien in a county "from the time there is filed in the office of the clerk of the court of common pleas of such county a certificate of such judgment". — [R.C. 2329.02](https://codes.ohio.gov/ohio-revised-code/section-2329.02)
- **Unverified states.** Recording is required in CA (abstract, CCP 697.310), TX (abstract with county clerk, Prop. Code ch. 52), FL (certified copy in Official Records, § 55.10), IL (memorandum of judgment, 735 ILCS 5/12-101), CO (transcript, CRS 13-52-102) and GA (general execution docket). NY and NJ lien by county-clerk or Superior Court docketing. These come from statute knowledge, verified: false. — repo `config/sol_proposed.yaml`; [W&W chart](https://www.whiteandwilliams.com/assets/htmldocuments/Judgment%20Liens%20-%20Rev%201-20-20.PDF)

### Inferences
- **Where to source each kind of state:**
  - Recording states (AZ, ID, NV, UT, MI, VT, DC and probably CA, TX, FL, IL, CO, TN): a recorder index is required to know whether a court judgment is a lien. A judgment with no recorded abstract is unsecured against real estate. This is why the Florida Official Records feeds (Hillsborough, Hernando and Putnam, already loaded) and Texas abstract-of-judgment indexes matter.
  - Docketing states (NC, SC, SD, ND, MN, IA, KS, MO, NE, WA, VA, MD, DE, WI, NY, NJ): the court's judgment index is enough. In VA the judgment lien docket is kept by the circuit clerk per county, and in MN by the court administrator per county. A clerk extract is both the judgment and the lien source.
  - Ohio: the source is the common pleas clerk's certificate-of-judgment index, not the recorder.
  - ME, MA, NH, RI: no lien exists without a recorded execution or attachment, so a docketed judgment alone does not encumber the debtor's equity.
- The model should store `lien_perfected_on` separately from `entered_on`. In recording states the lien clock often starts at recording: NV 6 years from docketing, MI 5 years from recording, ID 10 years from recording. Elsewhere it starts at entry: AZ 10 years after the date given, NC, SC and IA 10 years from entry or judgment, ND 20 years from docketing.
