# Open-network sweep of US open-data catalogs (2026-11-23)

Network was unrestricted for this pass, so this is the first sweep that actually fetched rows (counts, sums, samples) from the Socrata, CKAN and ArcGIS hosts that earlier sweeps could only see through catalog records. Everything already loaded (`config/liens/*.yaml`, `config/courts_federal/*.yaml`, `config/courts_state/*.yaml`) or already ranked/rejected in the two reports and the ten earlier notes files was skipped. The exception: datasets that earlier notes tagged `[CATALOG]`, `[SEARCH]` or "qualifying but host blocked" were never verified, so they were re-checked here and are included.

## Method and coverage

- **Socrata Discovery API** (`api.us.socrata.com/api/catalog/v1`, all domains, paged to 2,000 per term): the 51 terms in the brief plus 61 more ("amount due", "unpaid fines", "tax warrant", "nuisance lien", "demolition lien", "grass cutting", "hearing officer", "dangerous building", "tax deed" and others). **7,142 unique datasets**; about 210 relevant, updated in 2025 or later and not already known, were reviewed by hand.
- **data.gov**: the CKAN API (`catalog.data.gov/api/3/action/package_search`) is **retired and returns 404**. The new catalog serves JSON at `https://catalog.data.gov/search?q=<term>&per_page=100&after=<cursor>`. 44 terms gave **3,775 unique datasets**. Most local items were Socrata or ArcGIS copies, already covered.
- **Other CKAN portals** (`/api/3/action/package_search`): data.boston.gov, data.milwaukee.gov, data.wprdc.org, data.houstontx.gov, data.sanantonio.gov, data.ca.gov, data.virginia.gov, data.chhs.ca.gov, data.cnra.ca.gov, data.ok.gov, phoenixopendata.com and open.toronto.ca, each with 13 terms.
- **ArcGIS Online** (`www.arcgis.com/sharing/rest/search`, `type:"Feature Service"`, up to 400 results per term): 64 terms, many of them new phrasings ("unpaid balance", "lot clearing", "code lien", "municipal lien", "struck off", "adjudicated property", "certified delinquent", "utility lien", "board up", "receivership"). **11,710 items**. Of these, 1,144 relevant items from 2025 or later were probed automatically (service, layers, fields, count), and **414 layers** had an amount field plus a name or parcel field. Survivors were checked by hand.
- **Socrata counts and sums** use `/resource/<id>.json?$select=count(*),sum(<amt>)&$where=<amt>>0`. ArcGIS uses `returnCountOnly` and `outStatistics`.

Headline: the open catalogs are close to tapped out for **true named money judgments**. The one real new judgment-grade source is **Chicago DOE Environmental Enforcement**: administrative-hearing liability findings with named respondents. The other new rows are recorded or assessed municipal charges and tax delinquencies. Several delinquency lists that were blocked before (Milwaukee, Richmond, Norfolk, Clermont OH, Hamilton OH) are now verified and carry owner names, mailing addresses and parcel ids.

## Per-dataset notes

1. **Chicago DOE Environmental Enforcement (`yqn4-3th2`)**: the best new find and the only true judgment set. These are City of Chicago Department of Administrative Hearings (DOAH) findings on Public Health environmental tickets: liable after hearing (`L`), liable by plea, default, guilty. Each row names the `respondent` (businesses and LLCs, some people) with the violation address and `docket_no`. There is no paid or balance field, so the fine imposed is an upper bound. The loaded `6br9-quuz` (DOAH Ordinance Violations) is a different department's caseload, so this one is additive. A DOAH default or liability order can be entered as a judgment in Cook County Circuit Court. Match `respondent` and `address` to Cook owner names. Endpoint: `https://data.cityofchicago.org/resource/yqn4-3th2.json?$where=disposition in('L','LIABPLEA','DEFAULT','LIABLE','LIABCONT','G') AND fine_amount>0`.
2. **TCEQ Administrative Orders (`u66a-qggj`)**: this is the commission-order counterpart to the loaded `tx.tceq.civil_judgments` (`7ift-kbgd`). Agreed and default orders carry an assessed penalty, a deferred part and a payable amount. Respondent names only, and `county` is often `*MULTIPLE`. There is no payment status, so the $106M is an upper bound. The sibling `8j6x-idf8` (SEPs) adds nothing. Names can be matched against TX StratMap owner names, though common names will need care.
3. **Chicago Vacant and Abandoned Buildings – Violations (`kc9i-wq85`)**: DOAH and circuit cases with full money columns (fines, admin costs, interest, collection costs, court cost, paid, current due). The defendants are mostly lenders and REO servicers, so only 178 rows still owe ($1.39M). The data stops at 2025-10. It is small but clean, and negative balances show overpayments.
4. **WA PDC Enforcement (`a4ma-dq6s`)**: campaign-finance penalties against candidates and committees, with `balance_due` and `sent_to_collections`. It names people, but there is no address and WA has no parcel owners in the repo. Low value.
5. **Mesa Code Compliance (`hgf6-yenu`)**: carries APN (`parcel_number`) and a balance, but no owner name. Most balances sit on closed cases. Maricopa parcels are not loaded (`docs/coverage.md` lists Maricopa county layers as "to do"), so it is not linkable yet.
6. **Philadelphia L&I Outstanding Fees and Fines**: a daily feed of unpaid violation fines and re-inspection fees by L&I case. The only location key is `addressobjectid`, which must be joined through L&I's address layer (Carto `violations` or `case_investigations` carry `opa_account_num` with the same case numbers) to reach OPA and `pa.philadelphia.parcels`. A join on `casenumber` to Carto `violations.casenumber` should give `opa_account_num` and `opa_owner`. Endpoint: `https://services.arcgis.com/fLeGjb7u4uXqeF9q/arcgis/rest/services/outstanding_case_fees/FeatureServer/0/query?where=1=1&outFields=*&f=json`.
7. **NOLA Code Enforcement Active Pipeline (`8pqz-ftzc`)**: $48.8M `unpaidfees` across 15,148 cases, with GeoPIN. It probably overlaps the loaded `wy29-i338` adjudication cases. Use it to add GeoPIN to those cases rather than as a new source. Orleans parcels are not loaded.
8. **Cincinnati Private Lot Abatement (`pk9w-99n6`)**: city cleaning invoices billed to owners. Unpaid balances are certified to the county tax duplicate, which makes them a lien. Address only, so join on address to Hamilton parcels (OH statewide or #12).
9. **Independence MO Special Assessments**: small, but each row has a city-assessed lien amount, owner name, owner mailing address and Jackson County parcel number (in `Name`). It joins directly to `mo.jackson.parcels`. Endpoint: `https://gisportal.indepmo.org/giscoi/rest/services/ComDev/Special_Assessments_Public_Lyr/FeatureServer/8/query?where=total_current_balance IS NOT NULL&outFields=*&f=json`. `total_current_balance` is a string field, so `>0` fails and `IS NOT NULL` works.
10. **Milwaukee Delinquent RE Tax Accounts**: the earlier notes had this as `[SEARCH]` only; it is now verified. Monthly XLSX in the CKAN datastore, with SQL available (`https://data.milwaukee.gov/api/3/action/datastore_search_sql?sql=SELECT ... FROM "8f1367e1-6f8f-44cc-8ed6-2eecd8267ec7"`). It carries owner name, full mailing address, tax key and principal by levy year. All fields are text, so cast the amount. The delinquent personal-property file (`098495f3-0ef3-4e32-bb78-cc11eb1c024f`, 2,350 rows, $0.66M) names businesses but is not about real property. The MPROP MapServer (`milwaukeemaps.milwaukee.gov/arcgis/rest/services/property/parcels_mprop/MapServer`) is reachable now, with a "Tax status" layer (9) and foreclosed-property layers (12, 13, 23).
11. **Richmond VA (`83t5-hbac`)**: owner names and `property_code` (Richmond PIN), with `total_due` as a currency string. Cast with `replace(replace(total_due,'$',''),',','')::number`. Updated twice a year. It joins to the loaded Richmond city parcels.
12. **Hamilton Co OH parcel layer**: the earlier notes said this layer has no delinquency field. In fact it has `DELQ_TAXES`, `DELQ_TAXES_PD`, `DLNQDT`, `CNTDUE` (contract due) and `FORECL_FLAG`. Count is exactly 126,000 (Hamilton has about 350k parcels), so check whether the feature collection is partial before using it. `FORECL_FLAG='Y'` returned 0.
13. **Clermont `q52y-wzm4`**: verified, with clean owner, mailing address, parcel id and certified delinquent year. Public Domain.
14. **Norfolk VA (`7qie-z5gv`)**: one row per installment. `account` is the RE account, not GPIN. Norfolk's assessment datasets (`qva7-tzrf` FY27 and others) carry `parcel_id`, `gpin` and owner and could bridge it, but Norfolk parcels are not in the repo. Nuisance-abatement liens are excluded by the publisher.
15. **Sonoma defaulted tax (`bp8v-uax7`)**: CA secured-roll defaults with mailing address but no owner name. Not linkable to owners in the repo.
16. **King Co RP receivables (`dkna-i698`)**: parcel id only. WA has no owner names in the repo. Amounts are zero-padded cents in text, so use `billed_amount::number/100`.
17. **Broward unpaid tax certificates**: a personal ArcGIS copy (`muhammad.elbagoury`, sibling `Bagoury_Personal`) of tax year 2023 only. Useful as a schema hint. The Broward Revenue Collection official feed would be the real source.
18. **Delaware DNREC**: 191 penalty orders ($19.9M) against named sites. Small, and DE has no owners in the repo.
19–20. **Clermont companions**: `94fc-2usj` reproduces #13 with full tax detail. `77ey-nevf` lists levied special assessments, not arrears.

## Notes for the loaders

- data.gov's CKAN API is gone. Any code calling `catalog.data.gov/api/3/action/*` will now get 404; the replacement is `catalog.data.gov/search?q=&per_page=&after=`.
- Every Socrata host from the earlier "blocked" list (Chicago, Mesa, WA, Sonoma, Clermont, King, Norfolk, Richmond, Texas, Cincinnati, Orlando, BRLA) answered normally from this network.
