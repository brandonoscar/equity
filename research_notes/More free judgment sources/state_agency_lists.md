## Q1. What concrete free bulk (file/API) state-agency debtor/lien sources exist, ranked by usefulness?

### Takeaway
Very few state agencies publish a true bulk file. The verified winners are New Jersey's two NJDOL Excel files (WALL and Wage Violation Watchlist: name + address + amount + final-judgment date, monthly) and, by search, Pennsylvania L&I's UC/workers'-comp non-compliance lien lists (XLSX) and Florida DOR's monthly Excel list of unsatisfied tax warrants/liens ≥$100k. Most "top delinquent" lists are small (100–500 names), many lack addresses, and several are search portals rather than files.

- "Top N" lists (CA, SC, DE, NJ-Tax) are small and skew to defunct businesses; useful as high-value flags, not as coverage.

## Q2. Workers' comp, UI, labor wage judgments, child support, AG, environmental, Medicaid

### Takeaway
Beyond NJ and PA, these categories are mostly search portals or small posters. A few environmental agencies (MI, IA, WI) publish real-property lien lists with addresses, which are the most "lien-on-real-property" of anything found outside tax.

### Cited Findings
- **Workers' comp:** FL DWC stop-work order database is a query portal (dwcdataportal.fldfs.com/SWOquery.aspx); a "POC Data" page offers proof-of-coverage downloads, SWO export unconfirmed — [FL DWC POC Data](https://dwcdataportal.fldfs.com/POCData.aspx) [SEARCH; host refused]. ### Inferences
- Environmental liens (MI, IA) are true liens on specific real parcels with addresses — rare and high quality, but tiny volume.
- Child-support "most wanted" lists are not worth building on.

### Gaps
- Medicaid/estate-recovery and state central-collection lists: no sources found.
- Wage-judgment lists for NY, IL, WA, OR, MA labor departments: not found (NY DOL hosts refused).
